For residents and building staff

Help that reaches the right person.

Rica Vista Tenant Help lets residents send a maintenance or emergency request in a few taps, and notifies the right staff right away — including after hours.

Residents can use the web portal or a simple phone app. Staff use a manager console to change who gets texted or called, and to keep a record of every request.

This is a working proof of concept with clearly labeled sample data, prepared for review. In this demo, sending a request opens a text on your phone; a production version would send automatically from a building number.

How a request moves

1

Resident reports it

A tenant chooses a unit and a category — a water leak, a lockout, a gate issue, or an emergency — and adds a short description.

2

The right people are listed

Routing rules decide who should be notified, and in what order. Numbers and roles are easy to change in the manager console.

3

A record is kept

Staff can acknowledge the request and update its status. The tenant can see that it was received and when it was resolved.
